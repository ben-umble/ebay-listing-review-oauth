Privacy policy

Personal eBay Listing Review is a personal tool used by its owner to prepare and review their own eBay listings.

The tool stores listing notes and, after the owner grants permission, an eBay OAuth token on the owner’s local computer. It does not collect, sell, share or use analytics on visitor data. It does not process buyer or customer data.

This static website does not use cookies, analytics or server-side storage.

To request deletion of locally stored application data, the owner can delete the local project and revoke eBay access from their eBay account.
